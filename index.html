Drug Schedules Info

Quick compliance guide for Indian pharmacy dispensing.

  • Schedule H: Prescription only. Cannot be sold without a registered medical practitioner's Rx.
  • Schedule H1: High-risk antibiotics/habit-forming. Must maintain a separate register (Patient, Doctor, Qty). Retain records for 3 years.
  • Schedule X: Narcotics & Psychotropic drugs. Requires a duplicate prescription. Strict inventory logs required.
  • Schedule G: Medical supervision required. Carries a warning label.
